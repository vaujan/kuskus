On your computer.
Under your control.

Kuskus keeps your library in an ordinary folder you choose. You don’t need an account or a hosted library to collect and organize your references.

Notes for the whole process

Write in plain Markdown with highlighting and a reading view beside the editor. Your notes save locally as you work.

Local by default

Your saved notes and files are available offline. Opening websites and fetching link previews still use the internet.

Files you can keep

Open your notes, images, and documents outside Kuskus. Back up your library by copying its folder.

An open-source foundation

We’ve chosen GPL-3.0 for Kuskus. The public source release is being prepared, so you’ll be able to inspect, modify, and share the app.
